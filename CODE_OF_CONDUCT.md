# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, technical collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Focus discussion on code and behavior, not individuals.
- Provide evidence when raising claims.
- Accept review feedback in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Deliberate disruption of discussion or review processes

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, lock, or remove content that violates this policy.
Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
